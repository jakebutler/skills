# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is an experienced solo engineer coordinating several model families
across real repositories. They need to understand who is doing what, why a route was
chosen, whether work is still progressing, and what happens when a provider is out of
quota or unavailable. Agents are a secondary audience: the artifact must make the
operating contracts unambiguous enough to execute.

## Product Purpose

The AI Engineering OS turns a collection of skills, agents, hooks, workflows, and model
subscriptions into a dependable engineering harness. Success means the user can inspect
the system, predict its behavior, recover from provider failure without losing work, and
share the template across repositories without giving up human control.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, candid. The interface should feel like a well-made field manual: dense
enough for serious work, quiet enough to read, and explicit about uncertainty and
unfinished state.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI dashboards, ornamental gradients, glowing agent diagrams, chatty
status copy, indistinguishable card grids, and any interface that hides routing or
failure behind a vague loading state. Do not imply that work is running when the
underlying process has failed or stopped.

## Design Principles

1. Show the operating model, not a marketing abstraction.
2. Make route choice, ownership, and fallback behavior inspectable.
3. Preserve evidence and partial work across retries and provider changes.
4. Keep the user oriented with phase boundaries and honest status.
5. Teach progressively: overview first, contracts and evidence on demand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Do not rely on color alone for meaning. Support keyboard navigation,
visible focus, reduced motion, readable line lengths, and layouts that remain usable on
small screens and at 200 percent zoom.
