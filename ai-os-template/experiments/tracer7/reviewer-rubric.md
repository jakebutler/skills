# Tracer 7 blinded candidate rubric

Review only the assigned neutral candidate label. Do not infer or request model,
runtime, producer surface, provider, transcript, duration, route order, or sibling
output. The comparison is a routing-stack experiment, but route metadata remains
blinded until every score, finding, and disposition is frozen.

1. Reproduce D0 and candidate identities at start.
2. Enumerate all reachable effect surfaces before judgment and reconcile them with the
   frozen inventory and scoped invariant selection.
3. Return a binary gate verdict for every assigned requirement and invariant:
   satisfied, violated, or not-verifiable, with executable evidence. Do not substitute
   a holistic score for row-level coverage.
4. Findings require stable IDs, severity, file:line, concrete failure, reproduction,
   and fix direction. Separate design incompleteness from implementation defects.
5. Reproduce the candidate identity at finish. A mismatch invalidates the packet.

Score independently before seeing another lens: deterministic correctness, security
and authority preservation, architecture fidelity, test quality, scope discipline,
maintainability, and residual confidence. Do not reward stylistic familiarity. Timing
is not review evidence and may not be attributed to a model after unblinding.
